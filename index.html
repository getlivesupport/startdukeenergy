Duke Energy start, stop and move service overview

Moving is easier when you separate the three utility tasks: start Duke Energy service at the new address, stop Duke Energy service at the old address, or transfer Duke Energy service between eligible addresses. The official Duke Energy account flow is the best place to submit a request and confirm requirements for your service territory.

Quick answer: Go to the official Duke Energy Start, Stop & Move page, select the action you need, and keep the confirmation number. For general help with this guide, call 888-620-0950.

This independent information page is not Duke Energy. The number above is a third-party assistance line; for account-specific matters, use Duke Energy's official website or the number shown on your bill.

How to start Duke Energy service at a new address

Start service when you are moving into a home, apartment, rental, or business location that requires a new electric account. Submit the request as early as practical because availability, identity verification, deposits, and access to the meter can affect timing.

  1. Confirm the service address.
    Use the complete street address, apartment or unit number, city, state, and ZIP code. Avoid guessing the address from a listing.
  2. Choose Start Service.
    Use the official Duke Energy start/stop/move flow and follow the prompts for your location and account type.
  3. Choose the requested start date.
    Use the date you need electricity available, not merely the date you sign a lease. Ask the property manager whether power is currently on.
  4. Review requirements and submit.
    Have contact details and any requested verification or payment information ready. Save the confirmation and requested appointment window.
  5. Prepare for move-in.
    Take a meter or move-in photo if permitted, keep your confirmation, and test essential circuits after service is active.

How to stop Duke Energy service when moving out

Request a stop when you are leaving a property and will no longer be responsible for the account. Do not assume a landlord, buyer, or roommate will close your account unless you have written confirmation of the handoff.

  • Verify the exact address and account you want to close.
  • Select a final service date that matches your lease, closing, or responsibility date.
  • Provide a forwarding address for the final bill or deposit correspondence.
  • Record the final meter reading and take a dated photo when safe and allowed.
  • Save the confirmation and check the final bill after the service ends.
Important: Stopping service may not cancel a balance already owed. Review the final bill, scheduled payments, and any deposit-credit instructions separately.

How to transfer or move Duke Energy service

Use the move or transfer option when you are relocating and both addresses may be within a Duke Energy service area. A transfer normally involves closing or scheduling the old address and opening or scheduling the new address, so verify both dates carefully.

Old address

Confirm the stop date, final reading, forwarding address, and any access instructions.

New address

Confirm the start date, unit number, meter access, and whether power is currently on.

After submission

Save both confirmations and check your online account or email for follow-up requests.

If the new address is outside the eligible service territory, you may need to stop the old account and contact the new utility separately. The official portal will provide the applicable path.

Duke Energy move-in and move-out checklist

  • Service address and unit number
  • Start, stop, or transfer date
  • Government-issued identification or verification details if requested
  • Phone number and email address
  • Forwarding or billing address
  • Lease, closing, or occupancy details when applicable
  • Meter location and access instructions
  • Payment method or deposit information if requested
  • Confirmation numbers and screenshots

Never email sensitive personal information to an unverified contact. Use the official Duke Energy website or a secure account channel for account details.

Common Duke Energy start, stop and transfer problems

“My address is not found”

Check spelling, unit formatting, city, and ZIP code. A newly built property may not yet be indexed. Ask the property manager for the recognized service address and contact the official utility channel if the issue continues.

“I need service today”

Same-day availability is not guaranteed. Submit the request immediately, review any appointment options, and ask about access requirements. Do not rely on a verbal promise without a confirmation.

“The previous resident still has power”

Power may remain on during a change of occupancy. Submit your own start request and confirm the responsibility date rather than interrupting service or altering the meter.

“I received a deposit or verification request”

Follow the secure instructions displayed in your official account or on the official Duke Energy website. If a message looks suspicious, do not click unknown links; navigate directly to the official site.

“My final bill is wrong”

Compare the final bill with your stop date, meter photo, payments, and forwarding address. Contact official Duke Energy customer service using the details on your bill for an account-specific review.

Duke Energy Start, Stop or Transfer FAQs

How do I start Duke Energy service?

Open the official Start, Stop & Move page, choose Start Service, select your location, and follow the account and address prompts. Have your move-in date and service address ready.

How do I stop Duke Energy service?

Use the official account flow, choose Stop Service, verify the address, select the final service date, and save the confirmation for your records.

Can I transfer Duke Energy service to a new address?

If both addresses are in an eligible Duke Energy service territory, use the Move or Transfer option. Confirm the old stop date and new start date before submitting.

What information do I need to start service?

Typically you should have the service address, requested start date, contact information, forwarding details, and any verification or deposit information requested during the secure process.

How early should I request utility service before moving?

Request it as soon as your move date is firm. Timing varies by location, account status, verification, holidays, and whether an appointment or meter access is needed.

Who should I contact for account-specific help?

Use Duke Energy's official customer-service page or the phone number printed on your bill. For general navigation help with this independent guide, call 888-620-0950.
